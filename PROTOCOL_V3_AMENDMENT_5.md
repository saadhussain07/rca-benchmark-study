# Amendment 5 to protocol v3: pre-registered tests on RE3 of three RE2 exploratory findings

**Date:** 2026-10-12. Written and committed **before any run described here**.

**What had been seen:** every RE2 result, including the exploratory analyses of Amendments 1, 3 and 4, and the RE3 results of Amendment 3 for the conditions run there (`annotated`, `stripped`, `decoy`, `placebo`, `rep2`, and the RCAEval baselines).

**What had not been seen on RE3:**
- no baseline-aware log alarms;
- no `anon_full`;
- no onset shift, for any method.

The three hypotheses below were generated by looking at RE2. They are tested here on RE3 conditions that have not been run, which turns three exploratory RE2 findings into pre-registered tests. RE2 is not re-tested.

## Hypotheses, predictions and decision rules

All tests use the 90 RE3 cases, scored as in section 7 (chance for unflagged or unranked cases):
- cluster bootstrap (2,000 resamples) and cluster sign-flip permutation (10,000), seeded;
- clusters are (system, root service, fault);
- the deployed model is `gpt-oss-120b`, `annotated`, one vote, same settings.

| # | Hypothesis (from the RE2 finding) | Statistic | Supported if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H5a | Suppressing log alarms that already fired before the injection raises the deployed model's AC@1 more than it raises the pipeline's detector count. On RE2: model +0.159, detector count +0.007. | per-case difference in differences: (model with filter − model without) − (detector count with filter − without) | mean > 0 and Holm p < 0.05 |
| H5b | Hiding every spelling of every service name does not lower the deployed model's AC@1. On RE2: +0.033, and +0.094 / +0.057 for the other models. | `anon_full` − `annotated`, 95% CI | lower bound > −0.05 (non-inferiority, the section 7 margin) |
| H5c-1 | With the onset placed 120 s late, N-Sigma loses more AC@1 than the deployed model. On RE2: N-Sigma −0.730, model −0.115. | per-case (N-Sigma loss) − (model loss) | mean > 0 and Holm p < 0.05 |
| H5c-2 | The same for the pre-registered SimpleRCA-style heuristic. On RE2: −0.581 vs −0.115. | as H5c-1 | as H5c-1 |

- **Holm family:** H5a, H5c-1 and H5c-2 (three tests).
- **H5b** is a non-inferiority rule and is outside the family. It is reported for the other two models as descriptive.
- A null result is reported as prominently as a supported one.
- **Power:** with about 30 clusters, the smallest detectable difference is about 14 points (section 7 table). The RE2 effects behind H5c are far larger than that, while those behind H5a and H5b are smaller. A failure of H5a or H5b may therefore reflect power.
- **Sensitivity:** the same tests on the 84 cases outside `pilot_re3.txt`.

**A note on H5a.** RE3 may contain no always-on alarm comparable to Sock Shop's `queue-master`. If the filter changes nothing, H5a is not supported, and the paper says so. The rule is not altered after the fact.

## Runs

**Part A** (`scripts/run_amendment5_a.sh`; Groq; does not use RCAEval's `output/`):
1. `anon_full` on `out/re3` for all three models. 78 flagged cases each.
2. `03_build_contexts.py --suite re3 --chronic-filter` into `out/re3_chronic`, then the deployed model, `annotated`.
3. For S = −120 and +120: `03_build_contexts.py --suite re3 --onset-shift S` into `out/re3_shiftS`, then `05_classifiers.py`, `13_simplerca_variants.py --onset-shift S`, and the deployed model, `annotated`.

**Part B** (`scripts/run_amendment5_b.sh`, after part A has built the shifted folders): RCAEval N-Sigma and BARO on `re3-ob`, `re3-ss` and `re3-tt` with `--tdelta S`, kept in `output/kept_re3_shiftS`, then collected into `out/re3_shiftS` with `07_collect_rcaeval.py`.

**Analysis:** `scripts/15_amendment5.py --ctx out/re3 --pilot out/pilot_re3.txt` writes `out/re3/amendment5.md` and `.json`, together with a descriptive onset table for RE3.

**Calls:** at most 78 × 3 (`anon_full`), plus the flagged cases of three rebuilt folders for the deployed model, about 480 calls in all.
